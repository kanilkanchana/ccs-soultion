This release gives phones a dedicated video player with its own playback clock.
On phones: the 30-second mobile film plays sequentially through all six chapters,
with chapter text synchronized to the video's playback time. Page scrolling
does not scrub, seek, or skip the chapters. On scrolling out of view, the film
pauses until the user returns. After completing chapter 06 it stops (no loop).
A Replay button appears at the end, and a Play button appears if the browser
blocks muted autoplay.

On PCs and desktops: no change to the video, scrolling, or visual layout.
