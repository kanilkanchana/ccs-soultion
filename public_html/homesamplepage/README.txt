CCS SOLUTIONS — TOYOTA PRIUS 2025 CINEMATIC WEBSITE
==================================================

START HERE
----------
1. Extract the ZIP to a folder.
2. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, or Firefox, or upload all files to your web host.
3. Scroll down slowly. The background film follows your scroll position.
4. Click any chapter on the bottom timeline to jump to its scene.
5. Use WATCH THE FILM in the opening chapter for normal uninterrupted playback.
6. After the six chapters, the page continues into the CCS Solutions services website.

WEBSITE EDITS
-------------
- Open index.html in a text editor to change business wording, services, or links.
- Replace the wordmark with your official CCS logo image if desired. An official logo
  file was not included among the current uploads; the text brand is a design placeholder.
- The social links are the previously supplied Facebook and TikTok links.
- No unprovided phone number or location street address was invented.
- For faster hosting use assets/prius-30s-web.mp4 (as already configured).
- To switch to higher resolution, replace the video src in index.html with
  assets/prius-30s-master.mp4 (more network bandwidth required).

TECHNICAL AND ACCURACY NOTES
----------------------------
The film uses supplied AI-generated videos; it is cinematic illustration, not mechanically
verified Toyota 3D engineering CAD. Physical battery and engine geometry, scale, and
energy routing must not be used for repairs or technical training. Exact 360-degree
rotation and interactive opening parts would require a rigged 3D vehicle model.

Scroll controlled video depends on support for MP4 seeking. For optimal performance,
serve the site from a web server with HTTP byte-range support (standard static hosting
usually provides it). The website includes the first scene poster as fallback.
