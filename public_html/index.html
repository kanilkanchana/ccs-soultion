+ A FEW CLEAR ANSWERS

GOOD TO
KNOW.

Every vehicle is different.
Let’s start with your questions.

Do I need a full battery replacement?

That depends on the battery’s condition and inspection findings. CCS Solutions offers health testing, module replacement, reconditioning and complete replacement. An inspection helps establish which options suit your vehicle.

Can you help with an ABS warning light?

ABS diagnosis, module and pump repair or replacement, sensor replacement and brake-system repair are included in the service range. Contact the workshop with your vehicle model and symptoms to arrange an inspection.

How much will the repair cost?

Pricing depends on the fault, vehicle model, parts condition and work required. Share your details through the enquiry form to request a quote. Repair costs and timing should be confirmed with the workshop.

What warranty is available?

Warranty terms depend on the service or part supplied. Ask CCS Solutions to confirm the applicable period, distance limit and conditions for your chosen repair or replacement.
